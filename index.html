Tutorial

Welcome, Little Detective!

Now you're going to be a detective. Your task is to learn how to tell the real ones from the fake ones while playing with your friends. Let's take a look at how to play.

  • This board game can be played by 2 to 4 people.
  • Each player uses one character as a token on the game board.
  • Spin the wheel to see which colour challenge you'll face.
  • As you land, you'll get one of four colours — red, orange, blue, or green — each with a different challenge.
  • Red — face an "Ice Breaking" challenge with unique and exciting tasks.
  • Orange — face an "AI Deepfake" challenge, guessing whether an image was made by AI or a human.
  • Blue — face a "Misinformation" challenge, deciding if information is true or an AI-generated hoax.
  • Green — face an "AI Language" challenge, guessing if a sentence was written by a human or AI.
  • After receiving your challenge, answer it and share your reasoning with your friends, then discuss it together if needed.
  • Continue taking turns until someone reaches the finish line!

Policy

House Rules & Fair Play

  • Be kind — this game is about learning together, not winning at all costs.
  • Answer honestly before checking with the group. Guessing out loud is part of the fun!
  • If a challenge sparks a disagreement, talk it through — that's the whole point of media literacy.
  • Take turns fairly. If the "tap together" chance ever feels stuck, hit Try Again.

Privacy

  • This game runs entirely in your browser. No accounts, no tracking, no data ever leaves your device.
  • Nothing is saved after you close the tab — every new game starts fresh.
